HOW TO READ THIS PAGE

Evidence strength is multidimensional

PopulationCells, animals, healthy volunteers, or patients?
DesignControlled, randomized, blinded, observational, or uncontrolled?
SizeDoes the study include enough participants to support the conclusion?
ReplicationHas the finding been reproduced independently?
EndpointWas a meaningful clinical outcome measured, or only a surrogate?
UncertaintyWhat remains unknown despite the result?
